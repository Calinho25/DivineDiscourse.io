For youth groups and study circles

Use it as a 10-minute apologetics warmup.

Divine Discourse works well before a Bible study, RCIA session, youth meeting, or theology discussion. Pick one objection, let everyone respond, then compare the strongest points together.

Try this format

  1. Choose one objection.
  2. Give everyone five minutes to answer.
  3. Review feedback and discuss what was persuasive.
